## Risk and content review

- [ ] Explains what the exposure is and how it can fail.
- [ ] Compares a realistic alternative, including no action where appropriate.
- [ ] Covers fees, spreads, tax, liquidity, time and implementation burden.
- [ ] Labels currency units, including AUD/USD direction.
- [ ] Separates allocation, exposure and capital at risk.
- [ ] Recomputes numerical examples; identifies hypothetical assumptions.
- [ ] Addresses drawdowns, leverage, path dependence and stress correlations.
- [ ] Gives no guaranteed returns or unsupported personalised recommendations.
- [ ] Dates and verifies tax, superannuation, market-process and product claims.
- [ ] States what evidence would reverse the conclusion.
- [ ] Checks citations, rights, licence notices and IU membership boundaries.
- [ ] Removes credentials, account identifiers and unnecessary personal details.

## Learning and operation review

- [ ] Objectives match the explanation, worked example and assessment.
- [ ] Incorrect answers explain the misconception, rather than just mark it wrong.
- [ ] Keyboard navigation, readable layout and small-screen presentation checked.
- [ ] PowerPoint and classroom/resource exports checked separately.
- [ ] A fresh-profile import/restore test reproduces the intended lesson.
- [ ] Published material carries version, review date and review triggers.
